Flood emergency guide

Keep this guide available on your phone. If conditions are dangerous, prioritize people and follow official directions.

Before flooding

  • Monitor official MDRRMO and barangay announcements whenever you have a connection.
  • Charge phones and power banks; keep essential medicines, IDs, cash, water, and your Go Bag ready.
  • Know your assigned evacuation location and agree on a family meeting point.
  • Move valuables and important documents above areas likely to flood, if it is safe.

If evacuation is announced

  • Leave promptly and use the route and destination given by local officials.
  • Bring your Go Bag and help household members who need assistance, if safe to do so.
  • Tell your out-of-area contact where you are going when communications allow.
  • Do not return until authorities say it is safe.

Avoid floodwater

  • Never walk, drive, or ride through floodwater; it may be deeper or faster than it looks.
  • Stay away from rivers, drainage channels, submerged roads, and damaged bridges.
  • Keep children away from floodwater and downed power lines.
  • If trapped by rising water, move to higher ground if safe and signal for help.

When communications are limited

  • Use your agreed meeting point and family contact plan; do not rely on one phone or route.
  • Conserve phone battery and use text messages when calls do not connect.
  • Check on neighbors who may need help only when doing so does not put you in danger.
  • Use a battery-powered radio if available and follow verified official information.
Safety first: Monitor readings and this saved guide do not replace instructions from emergency officials. Do not enter floodwater to retrieve belongings or assist someone; call for help using the local emergency contact information you have verified.
